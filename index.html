About Our System

The Gym Membership Management System is a website designed to make gym membership easier for customers and gym administrators.

System Features

Visitor Features

  • View gym information and facilities.
  • Explore gym photos and equipment.
  • Compare membership plans and prices.
  • Check opening hours and contact information.
  • View available trainers and gym services.

Member Features (Planned)

  • Create an account and log in.
  • View membership details and expiration dates.
  • Request new memberships and renewals.
  • Check the current number of people inside the gym.
  • View gym crowd levels: Low, Medium, or High.
  • View attendance and membership history.
  • Book personal training sessions.
  • Receive membership expiration reminders.
  • Update personal account information.

Admin Features (Planned)

  • Add, edit, and manage gym members.
  • Manage membership plans and prices.
  • Approve membership and renewal requests.
  • Record member check-ins and check-outs.
  • Monitor gym occupancy and crowd levels.
  • View membership and attendance reports.
  • Manage trainers and training sessions.
  • Publish gym announcements.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a simple and organized system that saves time, improves the gym experience, and makes membership management more efficient.
